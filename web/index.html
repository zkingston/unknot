§ unknot

Untangle knots with motion planning, building off the approach of Ladd & Kavraki (2004). Draw or load a knot diagram and watch it get untangled.

Source code: github.com/zkingston/unknot (web/, MIT).

Additional Credits

Hard unknots: Burton et al. (2024), after the original sources listed below; DeepMind’s from Applebaum et al. (2025). Known knots: The Knot Atlas (n.d.). Drawings: SnapPy. Type: Iosevka, SIL Open Font License 1.1. Algorithms: CREDITS.md.
